Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting a car with Gruzin Auto in Georgia.

What documents do I need to rent a car?
You need a valid passport or national ID, and a valid national or international driver's license. The driver must be at least 21 years old and have held a license for at least 1 year.
Is a security deposit required?
Yes, a small security deposit is pre-authorized or held when collecting the vehicle. It is fully refunded immediately upon returning the car in its original condition.
Can I pick up in Tbilisi and drop off in Batumi or Kutaisi?
Yes! We offer flexible one-way rentals between Tbilisi, Batumi, and Kutaisi airports or city branches.
What is the fuel policy?
We operate on a transparent "Full-to-Full" fuel policy. You receive the vehicle with a full tank of fuel and simply return it full.
How does insurance coverage work?
All rentals include standard comprehensive insurance (CDW and Theft Protection). Premium zero-deductible options are also available at checkout.
